| ADR-R-010 | PROPOSED | `noa.ledger.transfer/1` is the public wire form of a reference ledger transfer: six required string members (`amount`, `fromAccount`, `ledger`, `salt`, `toAccount`, `unit`), closed-world, one spelling per value, `fromAccount` distinct from `toAccount`, JCS-canonicalized to `paramsHash` with no domain tag, a complete and invertible six-row display, eleven stable per-member refusal codes in a normative order, and two pinned projection identities whose implementation digest is recomputable from the published package. A 128-bit `salt` member keeps the tuple from being confirmed from `paramsHash` alone. The unit is the closed enum {`XTS`}, scale 0. It is additive and disjoint from `noa.receipt/0.1` (ADR-R-001) and, consistent with ADR-R-004, publishes the rule for one declared `action.canonical` only. | ADR-R-011 | PROPOSED | The reference gate gains a PINNED trust mode: a persistent gate key loaded from a file it never creates, and approver, audit, epoch and quorum taken only from an operator-provisioned roster (`noa.gate-roster/1`). The roster is authenticated in `/1` by file discipline (absolute path with every symlink vetted, owner other than the gate's uid, mode, link count, ancestor walk; a gate running as root is refused), a printed digest and an optional digest pin; a high-water state file, read and written under a lock, refuses rollback. Decisions are checked against the gate's audience, the epoch and the sealed display's recipients in both modes; roster expiry, class and quorum in pinned mode. No fallback to the self-minted alpha root exists, and alpha keeps no display sealer. | ADR-R-012 | PROPOSED | The reference gate commits an EFFECT-OWNED action itself: `noa.ledger.transfer` is registered together with a static effect-owned table, and `POST /v1/holds/:holdId/commit` (owner-scoped, no body) hands the stored artifacts to an in-process effect owner. The owner defends against a hostile caller of its commit API (hostile input objects, a hostile sealer): it reads its input once, refuses re-entry for the whole commit, and, against the pinned trust root, re-checks the hold's boot (the stored identifier and the signed freeze time) and re-verifies the hold envelope (signature, tenant, gate, hold id, epoch, mode), the deferred and approval receipts (signatures, linkage, ALLOWED verdict, the held action), the execution grant and its bindings, the approver decision (at the grant's signed `issuedAt`), that the approval receipt is the deciding approver's under the decision keyring's key and that the bound display was sealed to that approver; it enforces uniqueness over the envelope, decision and grant keys (again just before the write), re-derives the params hash from the snapshot's canonical text, checks ledger, accounts and funds, signs the EXECUTED receipt and consumption from its own verified parses, verifies what it signed and that it is bound to this commit's instant, and writes one row whose write consumes the authority; a ledger refusal is an unsigned, terminal REFUSED row. One effect-owning engine serves a boot in a process. What the owner cannot check from its artifacts is listed in `NON-CLAIMS.md` §S9. The grant is withheld from every hold view until an EXECUTED row exists, and `reserve`/`report` refuse such actions. A hold records its boot identifier: `decide` and `commit` refuse a hold frozen by another boot. The owner exists only over a pinned trust root. The hold resolution keeps `HUMAN_APPROVED_INTENT_NOT_EXECUTION_BOUND`. Reference implementation only: the ledger is in memory, and the route and codes are not wire language (`NON-CLAIMS.md` §S9). | [docs/gate-effect-owner.md](docs/gate-effect-owner.md), `packages/gate/src/effect-owner.ts`, `packages/gate/src/engine.ts` |
